Day 1 Recall: The Four Factors

Every economy uses four factors of production: Land (natural resources → rent), Labor (human effort → wages), Capital (tools/equipment → interest), and Entrepreneurship (risk/innovation → profit). Today, you'll apply these concepts to real businesses and your own life.

Key Takeaway:

Every dollar of income comes from one of four sources: rent (owning land), wages (providing labor), interest (providing capital), or profit (taking entrepreneurial risk). Understanding this helps you make smarter decisions about education, career, saving, and potentially starting a business.
