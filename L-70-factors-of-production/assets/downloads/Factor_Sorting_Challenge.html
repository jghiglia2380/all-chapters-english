Factor Sorting Challenge

Activity 1: Classify resources into the four factors of production

📋 Instructions

Drag each resource to the correct factor of production category. Think carefully—some items might surprise you!

Remember the four factors:

  • 🌍 Land = Natural resources (oil, water, land)
  • 👷 Labor = Human effort (workers, professionals)
  • 🏭 Capital = Human-made tools (machines, buildings)
  • 💡 Entrepreneurship = Innovation, risk-taking, organizing

Resources to Sort:

Ocean fishing rights Factory robot arm Software developer Elon Musk's vision Texas crude oil Delivery truck Registered nurse Starting a food truck Sunlight for solar panels Cash register system Manager's expertise Risk of business failure

🌍 LAND

👷 LABOR

🏭 CAPITAL

Tools & equipment

💡 ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Innovation & risk

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid:

  • Robot as Labor: Robots are capital (human-made tools), not labor. Labor requires human effort.
  • Manager expertise as Entrepreneurship: Management skills are labor (human capital). Entrepreneurship is specifically about risk-taking and organizing a business.
  • Starting a business as Capital: The act of starting a business is entrepreneurship. Capital refers to the physical equipment purchased.
