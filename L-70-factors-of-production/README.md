## Content Highlights:

### Real-World Examples:

**{{STATE_NAME}} Restaurant Example:**
- Land: Downtown location ($3,000/month rent)
- Labor: 1 chef + 2 cooks + 4 servers + 1 manager ($15,000/month payroll)
- Capital: Kitchen equipment, POS, furniture, vehicle ($130,000 investment, $800/month interest)
- Entrepreneurship: Owner's vision, risk ($150,000 invested), monthly profit goal $5,000
- Total monthly math: Revenue $40,000 - Costs $34,000 = ~$5,000 profit (after materials/utilities)

**Coffee Shop Business Analysis:**
- Downtown location: $2,000-4,000/month rent
- 1 manager + 3 baristas (~$12,000/month payroll)
- Espresso machine ($15,000), equipment loans at 7%
- Average ticket $8, 150 customers/day = ~$31,200/month revenue

**Tech Startup Analysis:**
- Co-working space: $500-2,000/month (low land intensity)
- 2 developers ($70K each), 1 designer ($50K) = $10,000/month
- Computers/software: minimal capital compared to manufacturing
- SaaS model: 500 subscribers × $30 = $15,000/month revenue

**Family Farm Analysis:**
- 200 acres: significant land costs (rent ~$200/acre/year = $3,333/month)
- Family labor + seasonal workers
- Tractor, implements, irrigation ($100,000 investment)
- Crop yield: ~$400/acre × 200 = $80,000 annual revenue
