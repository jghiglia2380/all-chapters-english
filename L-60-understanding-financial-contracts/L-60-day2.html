Activity 4: Contract Negotiation Role-Play

⏱️ Time: 10 minutes

Objective: Practice negotiating contract terms and advocating for your interests.

Scenario 1: Apartment Lease Negotiation

Problematic Term: "Early termination requires 60 days notice and payment of two months' rent ($1,800)"

Your Situation: You accepted a job in another state starting in 10 weeks. You have 8 months remaining on your lease.

Prepare Your Negotiation:

Suggested Talking Points:

  • ✓ "I've been a reliable tenant (always paid on time, no issues)"
  • ✓ "I can help find/screen a replacement tenant"
  • ✓ "Could we reduce the fee to one month's rent instead of two?"
  • ✓ "I'm leaving for career advancement, not dissatisfaction with the property"
  • ✓ "Would you waive the fee if I find an approved replacement?"

Possible Compromise:

Pay one month's rent ($900) and forfeit security deposit ($900) = $1,800 total, but spread over time instead of upfront

Scenario 2: Employment Non-Compete Negotiation

Problematic Term: "Employee shall not work for any competing business within 50 miles for 2 years after leaving Company."

Your Situation: This is your dream job, but there are only 3 major employers in your field within 50 miles.

Reasonable Counter-Proposal:

Instead of: 2 years / 50 miles / any competing business

Propose: 1 year / 25 miles / direct competitors only (not entire industry)

This still protects the employer's interests while not completely restricting your career mobility.

🎯 Key Takeaways

✓ Reading contracts thoroughly before signing is non-negotiable - courts enforce contracts as written

✓ All valid contracts have five essential elements: offer, acceptance, consideration, capacity, and legality

✓ Fine print contains the most important terms - fees, penalties, termination conditions, rights you're waiving

✓ Contract comparison reveals significant differences - similar offers can vary by thousands of dollars

✓ Many terms are negotiable - professional negotiation often succeeds, especially for major contracts

✓ Common red flags: automatic renewal, unilateral modification, arbitration clauses, liability limitations, prepayment penalties

✓ Personal checklists ensure consistency - use your checklist for every contract

✓ Contract literacy is practical and powerful - saves thousands of dollars and prevents legal problems

✓ Professional advice matters - major contracts benefit from attorney review

✓ Your signature creates binding obligations - never sign under pressure or without understanding
