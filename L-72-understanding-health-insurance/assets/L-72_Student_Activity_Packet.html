Station 2: ACA Marketplace Navigator

Purpose: Learn that lower income = larger subsidies, and understand the metal tier system.

Key Information:

  • 2025 Federal Poverty Level (FPL): $15,060 for single adult; $20,440 for 2-person household; $30,900 for family of 4
  • Subsidy eligibility: Income between 100-400% FPL
  • Medicaid eligibility: In expansion states, adults up to 138% FPL may qualify
  • Metal Tiers: Bronze (~60% coverage), Silver (~70%), Gold (~80%), Platinum (~90%)

Station 3: HSA vs. FSA vs. Neither Decision Tool

Purpose: Understand when the HDHP/HSA combination makes mathematical sense.

Background:

  • HSA: Only available with HDHP. Triple tax advantage. Funds roll over indefinitely. 2025 limit: $4,300 individual.
  • FSA: Available with any non-HDHP plan. Pre-tax contributions but use-it-or-lose-it annually. 2025 limit: $3,200.
